# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Pre-Shipment Apparel Quality Control & Defect Mitigation

**Document ID:** SOP-WMS-QC-2026-V3  
**Applicability:** Fulfillment Centers · Packing Stations · Pre-Dispatch Quality Control Technicians  
**Owner:** Central Warehouse Quality Operations & Reverse Logistics Division

---

## 1. Objective and Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the mandatory physical inspection protocol for apparel parcels flagged with high fabric or construction defect risk prior to poly-bag sealing and dispatch label generation.

## 2. Threshold for Physical Inspection
Fulfillment packing stations operate on dynamic risk triggers generated by the Reverse Logistics Decision Support System (DSS). A parcel must be physically intercepted and routed to the Table-Level QC Technician when:
- The SKU's historical fabric defect score ($\phi_{\text{fabric}}$) exceeds **0.35**, or
- The customer review aspect analysis indicates recurring customer complaints regarding seams, zippers, or fabric transparency, and
- The Unit Economics Engine confirms that the ₹35.00 manual inspection cost yields positive net savings against the ₹400 baseline return loss.

## 3. Mandatory 4-Point Physical QC Checklist
Technicians executing the ₹35 Pre-Shipment QC Inspection must complete the following 4-point verification in under 90 seconds:
1. **Slider & Fastener Integrity Check:**
   - Fully zip and unzip all zippers twice. Verify no tooth misalignment, cloth pinching, or slider resistance.
   - For buttoned garments, ensure all buttons are cross-stitched with minimum 4 anchor points. Inspect spare button pouch.
2. **Stress-Point Seam Tensile Verification:**
   - Gently pull opposing fabric panels across shoulder joints, armholes, and crotch seams (for bottoms).
   - Any loose threading exceeding 5 mm or unraveled overlock stitching requires immediate unit rejection.
3. **Fabric Surface & Dye Uniformity:**
   - Inspect fabric against standard 4000K overhead inspection lighting.
   - Check for shade variation, weaving ladders, oil marks, or dye bleeding.
4. **Barcode & Size Label Concordance:**
   - Confirm that the physical sewn-in neck/waist tag matches the SKU barcode label on the garment polybag.

## 4. Defect Routing & Disposition Protocol
- **Minor Defect (e.g., loose hanging thread):** Trim with ceramic thread shears and proceed with packaging.
- **Major Defect (e.g., jammed zipper, broken seam, severe color blotch):**
  1. Scan barcode into WMS terminal with disposition code `QC_REJECT_DEFECT`.
  2. Drop the defective piece into the designated Vendor Return Bin (`BIN_VRB_04`).
  3. Pull an identical replacement SKU from prime pick storage (`BIN_A_PRIMARY`) within 3 minutes.
  4. Seal verified unit and approve dispatch.

## 5. Economic Justification
Catching a defective zipper or ripped seam prior to dispatch eliminates a guaranteed reverse logistics cycle:
- Averted Reverse Shipping Cost: ₹180.00
- Averted Inspection, Steaming & Repackaging Sunk Cost: ₹220.00
- **Total Averted Sunk Loss:** ₹400.00 per unit
- **Net Shift ROI:** $\text{Net Savings} = ₹400.00 - ₹35.00 (\text{QC Cost}) = +₹365.00$ per intercepted defect.
