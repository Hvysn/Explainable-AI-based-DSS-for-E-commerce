## 1. Scope
This document sets the operational standards for deploying reinforced protective packaging and navigating carrier Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with 3PL logistics partners (Blue Dart, Delhivery, Shadowfax, Xpressbees).

## 2. In-Transit Damage & Sunk Cost Dynamics
Transit damages account for 18% of all reverse logistics claims in emerging markets. Fragile embellishments, fine embroidery, and premium outer-wear face severe compression during long-haul surface transit to Tier-2 and Tier-3 pincodes.
- **Direct Carrier Compensation Limit:** Standard carriers only compensate up to ₹2,000 for lost parcels, with zero compensation for crushed packaging or surface abrasion unless protective sealing protocols are documented.
- **Customer Return Driver:** Over 42% of customers refuse delivery at the doorstep (RTO) if the external carton or polybag arrives crushed or torn.

## 3. Intervention Protocol: ₹20 Reusable Protective Packaging
Deploy the heavy-duty, reusable protective sleeve ($C = ₹20.00$) under the following conditions:
1. **Destination Geography:** Delivery to Tier-3 pincodes requiring $\ge 2$ hub sorting transshipments.
2. **Product MRP & Category:** Apparel items with gross cart value $> ₹2,500$ or items featuring delicate embroidery/mirror-work (e.g., Anarkali Suits, Silk Kurtis).
3. **Packaging Specification:** 
   - Multi-layer bubble-cushioned polymer pouch with tamper-evident security tape.
   - Puncture resistance $> 600\text{ kPa}$.

## 4. Cost-Benefit Matrix for Packaging Intervention
$$\text{Expected Net Savings} = \Big(\eta_{\text{pack}} \times P_{\text{return}} \times ₹400\Big) - ₹20.00$$
Where $\eta_{\text{pack}} = 0.40$ (eliminates 40% of doorstep refusal incidents caused by transit handling).

## 5. Carrier Return-to-Origin (RTO) Escalation Rules
If a carrier records $\ge 3$ consecutive delivery attempt failures without GPS scan proof, the warehouse supervisor initiates an automated carrier penalty deduction ticket under SLA Clause 9.4 and reroutes return sorting to the nearest regional cross-dock.
